Control the details

Keep your table
working for you.

Adjust your data as it changes, from the shape of the table to the way each column behaves.

01

Import and export Excel

Bring in an existing workbook with all its sheets, then export every sheet tab with columns in their current order.

02

Resize and hide columns

Drag a column header’s edge to give values room, or hide columns you don’t need. A red badge counts what’s hidden.

03

Change a column type

Switch types as your needs change. WebTalika previews values that cannot convert before you confirm.

04

Reimport and merge

Merge a workbook by matching sheets and columns by name, add its sheets as new tabs, or replace everything.

05

Reset the table

Clear all rows and columns with a confirmation before the reset is applied.

06

Filter any column

Filter ranges, pick Set values, or match text in any column. Filtered columns turn amber, and one click clears them all.

07

Work in sheet tabs

Keep related tables side by side in one document. Add, rename, duplicate, and reorder tabs, each with its own columns and filters.

08

Filter across tabs

Keep only the entries that match another tab: contains, equals, greater or less than, before or after. Add as many conditions as you need.

09

Pick a value to match

Compare against any value in the other tab’s column, or choose one of its values to filter by just that.
